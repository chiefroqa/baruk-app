Sea freight from China to Kenya: FCL, LCL and groupage

Sea freight is the most economical way to move heavy, bulky or large-volume cargo from China. Baruk offers full containers and shared space so smaller traders can ship too.

FCL: a full container

You book the whole container. It suits large orders, and your goods are not mixed with anyone else's.

LCL and groupage: share a container

We consolidate small orders into shared containers, so you pay only for the space your cargo takes. This removes the biggest barrier for small and growing traders.

Port to doorstep

We arrange clearance at the port and delivery to your premises, with status updates through the shipment.

What affects the price

Cargo volume or weight, product type, supplier location and clearance requirements. Your quote shows every component up front.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between LCL and FCL?

FCL means you rent a whole container. LCL means your goods share one with other shippers and you pay for the space used.

Can I ship a small order by sea?

Yes, through LCL or groupage. Send us the cargo details for a quote.

Do you handle clearance for sea cargo?

Yes. KRA, KEBS and IDF paperwork is part of the service.
