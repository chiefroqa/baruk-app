The cost of importing from China to Kenya

The price on the supplier's invoice is only part of what an import costs. This guide explains each cost so you can budget properly.

1. Product cost

What the supplier charges, usually quoted ex-factory or FOB. Sourcing and quality checks may add a fee.

2. Freight

Air freight is typically charged by weight, and sea freight by volume or container. Air is faster; sea is cheaper for heavy cargo.

3. Customs duty and taxes

Duty depends on the product's HS code under the EAC tariff. VAT is 16%, and levies such as the Import Declaration Fee and Railway Development Levy apply on the customs value. Rates are set by KRA and change, so always confirm current figures on the KRA site.

4. Clearing and delivery

Clearing agent charges, port or airport handling and delivery to your premises in Nairobi.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate import duty in Kenya?

Find the product's HS code, apply the duty rate to the customs value, then add VAT and the levies on top. KRA publishes the rates and a calculator.

Is it cheaper to import than to buy locally?

Often, but not always. Compare your landed cost with local prices, which is why we quote it in full.

Are there hidden costs?
