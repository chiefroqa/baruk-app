How to buy from Alibaba and ship to Kenya safely

Alibaba makes finding suppliers easy but gives no guarantee of quality or delivery. These steps reduce your risk.

1. Verify the supplier

Check the company's business details, trading history and reviews. Be cautious of prices far below the market.

2. Order samples first

Test quality before committing to volume, and agree specifications in writing.

3. Avoid risky payments

Do not send the full amount to an unverified supplier. Use protected payment options or a sourcing agent who pays after verification.

4. Plan freight and customs

Agree who ships, which route, and how Kenyan customs will be cleared. Price the full landed cost, not just the product.

5. Or use an agent
