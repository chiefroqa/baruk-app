LCL vs FCL shipping to Kenya: which should you choose?

Choosing between a shared and a full container affects your cost, timing and control. Here is how to decide.

FCL (full container load)

You rent a whole container. Your goods travel alone, which suits large volumes.

LCL (less than container load)

Your goods share a container with others, and you pay for the space you use. It suits smaller orders.

Groupage

Groupage is consolidation of several small shipments into one container, which is how small traders access sea freight.

How to choose

If your cargo fills most of a container, compare FCL. If not, LCL or groupage usually costs less overall. Send us your cargo details and we compare both.

Frequently asked questions

Is LCL slower than FCL?

Shared containers can take longer to consolidate and clear. We confirm timing in your quote.
